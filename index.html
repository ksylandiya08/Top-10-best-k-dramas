Top-10 best comedy K-dramas:

  1. The first Responders
    "The first Responders" tells the story of the cooperation of
    police, firefighters and paramedics, who are the first
    arrive at emergency calls. The Taewon Police Department is
    lost in conjecture: why was Lieutenant Jin Ho-ge, known as Dog
    Jindo, transferred to them? Jindo himself does not seem to
    mind the demotion at all - simply never, because the first
    case becomes literally hot...
  2. Undercover High School
    National Intelligence Agent Jeon Hye-sung is a true
    professional. But one day he failed to complete the task due
    to an unfortunate incident. That is why he was demoted and
    sent under the guise of a student to a high school, where he
    will have to find the emperor's gold bars. This is where the
    man's real adventures begin.
  3. The Trauma Code: Heroes on Call
    The story of a brilliant surgeon who joins a brigade of severe
    injuries in the hospital and returns her former glory. Pek Kang Hök is a brilliant traumatologist who does not want to compromise. He has conducted operations in conflict zones around the world, is confident of his power and behaves like a bulldozer when he thinks he is doing the right thing. Recently, Pak Kang Hök has led a team of traumatologists at the university hospital. But this brigade is a stick on two ends for the hospital. The fact is that the more serious patients the team saves, the more losses the hospital suffers. Yang Jae-won is a surgeon chosen by Pak Kang-Hök as his first student. Han Yu Rim is a medical professor who concealed the offense against Pak Kang-Hyok for "theft" of his ward Yang Zhe Wong. Chong Chang Mi is a seriously injured brigade nurse with a strong sense of responsibility. Park Hyun-won is a resident doctor who is stuck on all operations requiring anesthesiologist intervention, although he needs to prepare for examinations.
  4. Hit The Top
    Yu Hyun-je is a rising star of the 1990s. But because of his bad character, the singer is constantly in scandalous situations. When the idol of youth suddenly disappears without a trace, everyone decides that the idol of millions has died. However, Yoo Hyun-je mysteriously moves through time in 2017. He is looking for his friends, who have changed greatly over the years, and also meets two students who study for civil servants. One of them is Li Ji-hong, who is preparing for a civil servant exam, but secretly dreams of a musical career and wants to become an idol, which is supported by his loyal friend and classmate Choi Wu. After meeting new acquaintances, the guest from the past seriously thinks that if he really far ahead of his time and was here to help young talents and become part of their group? Maybe all together they can write their best hit.
  5. Mr. Queen
    The talented chef Chang Bon-hwan worked at the South Korean government residence when he mysteriously moved into the past and was imprisoned in the body of the Joseon Queen Kim So-yeon. Her husband is Chol Jung, who has been forced to relinquish control of the state to the late King's spouse and has no leverage to regain power. While the country is in turmoil for the throne between the reigning ruler and her younger brother, chef Bong-hwan, while in So-young's body, discovers that Chol Jung keeps dark secrets and intends to fight for what is rightfully his own…
  6. Twenty-five, Twenty-one
    Year 1998. High schoolgirl Na Hi-do is in a school fencing club, but when it is dissolved due to financial crisis, the girl manages to get to the national team on fencing sabres. Pack's father lost his business due to a difficult economic situation, and the family from a wealthy family turned into a poor one, and the boy was forced to go and earn money by delivering newspapers.
  7. The Uncanny Counter
    The secret organization Counter consists of people with superpowers who hunt evil spirits. They are forced to hide their activities, so they work in a small cafe, famous for its noodles. Chef Chu Me-ok can treat people, great "counter" Choi Jan-mul is engaged in accounting, psychic Do Ha-na searches for spirits and looks through memories, former police officer Ka Mo-thak has incredible strength, and senior Moon just prepares to learn his abilities and test them in business...
  8. From Now On, Showtime!
    Chha Chha Un is a popular, albeit somewhat sly, magician who creates spectacular performances on stage and on television. His tricks impress the public, but few people know - he has a team of secret assistants! However, these are not ordinary magician's assistants: they are actually ghosts. But Chha Chha Un is not afraid of creatures from the realm of spirits—he treats them as his employees…and they, in turn, consider him their "boss." One day, his paths cross with Go Sil He, a young, passionate policeman who can see ghosts, but is afraid of his abilities. She investigates an incident related to one of his magic shows. And she soon realizes that the unusual abilities of a magician can become an ideal tool in the fight against crime. She manages to convince him to join the police and help catch violent criminals using his ghost squad.
  9. Bora Debora
    The action revolves around Deborah, a talented writer who not only glorifies romantic relationships, but also gives various advice on how to choose a date, although she herself cannot succeed in her personal life due to constant conflicts with the guy. One day, the heroine accidentally encounters Lee Su-Heck, a successful employee of the publishing house, who has a negative attitude towards her work. Despite the differences related to professional activities, they are forced to work together, which unexpectedly leads to a passionate romantic relationship…
  10. Twinkling Watermelon
    Un-gel is the only deaf person in the family who studies at school and is obsessed with music. During the day he is a decent student, and in the evenings he performs in clubs as the guitarist of a rock band. Once in a mysterious musical instrument store, the high school student moves back to the past, in April 1995, where he meets his young father, I-chan, who is in love with a strapping cellist Se-ken, rather than with his future wife Chong-a. Intending to influence his father's choice, Un-Gel became a member of his musical group, while pondering how to return in 2023.
